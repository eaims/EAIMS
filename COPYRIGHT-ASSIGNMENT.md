# EAIMS Copyright Assignment — v1.0 Form

**Important:** This template is intended for legal review before operational use. Assignment effectiveness and moral-rights treatment vary by jurisdiction.

## 1. Parties and assigned contribution

This Copyright Assignment ("Assignment") is between the individual or entity identified below ("Assignor") and **Elias Naserkhaki** ("Assignee") for the contribution identified below ("Contribution").

## 2. Assignment

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Assignor hereby **assigns, transfers, and conveys to Assignee all right, title, and interest in and to the copyright and other assignable rights in the Contribution**, including all rights to:

- use, reproduce, publish, and distribute;
- modify, adapt, translate, edit, combine, and create derivative works;
- publicly display and perform;
- sublicense through one or more tiers;
- **relicense** under different terms;
- **dual-license** under open, source-available, commercial, or proprietary terms;
- commercially exploit and **proprietarily relicense** the Contribution;
- enforce the assigned copyright to the extent permitted by law; and
- exercise all corresponding transferable rights in any medium or format now known or later developed.

The assignment includes, to the extent legally assignable, rights in future causes of action arising from unauthorized exploitation of the assigned copyright after the effective date.

## 3. Fallback license where assignment is ineffective

If any right intended to be assigned cannot legally be assigned, or if the assignment of any right is held invalid or ineffective, Assignor grants Assignee, to the maximum extent permitted by law, a **perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, exclusive to the extent legally possible, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free, fully paid-up license** to exercise that right, including use, reproduction, modification, derivative works, distribution, sublicensing, relicensing, dual-licensing, commercial licensing, and proprietary relicensing.

## 4. Patent license

To the extent Assignor owns or controls patent claims necessarily infringed by authorized use of the Contribution as submitted, Assignor grants Assignee a **perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, transferable, sublicensable, royalty-free, fully paid-up patent license** to make, have made, use, offer to sell, sell, import, and otherwise transfer implementations of the Contribution. Assignee may sublicense those rights through one or more tiers, including in open, dual-licensed, commercial, or proprietary distributions.

## 5. Moral rights and consents

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Assignor waives and agrees not to assert moral rights, droit moral, attribution rights, integrity rights, or similar non-economic rights with respect to authorized use of the Contribution. Where waiver is not permitted, Assignor grants all consents legally available to permit modification, adaptation, sublicensing, relicensing, distribution, and other authorized exploitation.

## 6. Assignor representations

Assignor represents that:

1. Assignor owns or controls the rights being assigned or licensed and has authority to sign this Assignment.
2. The Contribution does not knowingly contain undisclosed employer-owned, client-owned, confidential, unlawfully copied, or restricted third-party material.
3. Assignor has disclosed known third-party licenses, patent claims, contractual restrictions, and other encumbrances affecting the Contribution.
4. No other agreement known to Assignor prevents the assignment or fallback license granted here.

## 7. No governance, joint-work, or official-role rights

This Assignment does not make Assignor an EAIMS author, co-author, steward, maintainer, governing authority, official representative, or release approver and does not create governance, veto, approval, licensing, or release rights. The parties do not intend the act of contribution or incorporation to create joint authorship or EAIMS co-authorship, joint ownership of EAIMS as a whole, co-stewardship, or shared governance.

## 8. Further assurances

Assignor agrees, upon reasonable request and without additional substantive compensation other than reimbursement of reasonable out-of-pocket costs, to execute documents reasonably necessary to confirm or perfect the rights assigned here.
