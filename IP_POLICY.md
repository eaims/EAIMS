# Intellectual Property Policy

## Independent expression

Contributions must be independently written. Ideas, facts, methods, and public principles may inform EAIMS, but contributors must not reproduce protected wording, tables, graphics, taxonomies, scoring rules, or other expression from proprietary frameworks, paid standards, employer materials, client work, or confidential sources.

## Rights to contribute

By submitting a contribution, each contributor certifies the Developer Certificate of Origin in [DCO.md](DCO.md) and licenses the contribution under the license applicable to the destination file. Contributors must identify third-party material and its license or permission.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI assistance must be disclosed when material. The contributor remains responsible for originality, factual review, rights clearance, confidentiality, and license compliance. Output must not be copied from inaccessible or proprietary source material.

## Standards and trademarks

Third-party standards may be cited accurately, but their protected text and logos must not be reproduced without permission. Compatibility, alignment, equivalence, certification, and endorsement claims require documented evidence and maintainer approval.

## Patents

The documentation license does not grant patent rights. Code licensed under Apache-2.0 carries only the patent terms stated in that license. Contributors should disclose any known patent claim they believe is necessarily implicated by their contribution.

## Reports

Potential infringement or ownership concerns may be reported privately using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove disputed material while reviewing provenance and rights.
