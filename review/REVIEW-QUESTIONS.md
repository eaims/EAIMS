# EAIMS 1.0 — Structured RC Review Questions

Review only the domains relevant to your expertise; mark others not reviewed.

## Architecture and scope

1. Do the 8 dimensions and 30 capabilities cover enterprise AI maturity without material duplication or omission?
2. Are any capabilities wrongly grouped, too broad, or too narrow?
3. Does EAIMS remain usable for small organizations while supporting complex/federated enterprises?

## Maturity model

4. Are L1–L5 anchors observably different for each capability?
5. Are L4 operational evidence and L5 adaptation expectations defensible?
6. Are there maturity claims that could be achieved by documentation without demonstrated operation?
7. Are any anchor prerequisites too strict or too weak?

## Evidence and assessment

8. Does the separation between maturity, evidence class, and confidence make sense?
9. Can evidence validity, invalidation, conflict, cutoff, and provenance rules be gamed or misinterpreted?
10. Is `INDETERMINATE` used appropriately rather than forcing low maturity?
11. Are Partial/Targeted assessment semantics sufficient to prevent overclaiming?
12. Does Assessment Quality remain adequately separate from organizational maturity?

## Governance / HAB / autonomy

13. Is the Human Accountability Boundary operationally meaningful?
14. Are Human Reserved decisions sufficiently distinct from autonomy levels?
15. Are A0–A5 autonomy categories understandable and governable in real enterprises?
16. Are agent permission, delegation, action-limit, monitoring, and suspension controls sufficient?
17. Are there situations where EAIMS would allow autonomy that should require stronger controls?

## Risk and gates

18. Are Impact, Reversibility, Scale, Velocity, Detectability, Likelihood, and uncertainty separated appropriately?
19. Are G0–G3 activation rules understandable and proportionate?
20. Could gate caps/blocks produce misleading maturity interpretations?
21. Are Approved Exceptions represented honestly without converting breaches into passes?

## Value and enterprise execution

22. Does Pilot-to-Scale governance sufficiently connect maturity to actual business execution?
23. Is Value Realization strong enough to prevent governance-only maturity inflation?
24. Are federated-enterprise semantics sufficient for holdings/groups/business units?

## Executability and reproducibility

25. Are the machine-readable specifications traceable enough to serve as canonical sources of truth?
26. Do executable rules preserve assessor judgment where automation would be misleading?
27. Are there written requirements that the current executable engine interprets incorrectly?
28. Are the three synthetic reference implementations challenging enough to expose design defects?
29. What additional negative/edge scenario should be mandatory before RC?

## Claims and research boundary

30. Are any public-facing claims stronger than the evidence currently supports?
31. What empirical study should have highest priority immediately after v1 RC?
32. Would you label the current candidate `ACCEPT_WITH_MINOR_CHANGES`, `REVISE_AND_REREVIEW`, or `NOT_READY_FOR_RC`, and why?
