## Post-independent comparison

Complete only after both assessors finish independently.

For each case record:

- exact agreement: yes/no;
- disagreement type:
  - activation;
  - requirement wording;
  - evidence threshold;
  - gate effect;
  - confidence only;
- resolution:
  - wording change;
  - protocol clarification;
  - worked-example clarification;
  - retain professional judgment;
- whether disagreement blocks candidate freeze.

## Calibration acceptance criterion

Candidate freeze should not proceed while a **critical** requirement or gate has unresolved disagreement caused by ambiguous EAIMS wording.

Differences caused only by explicitly retained professional judgment may remain if:

- the judgment boundary is documented;
- both interpretations are defensible;
- the result cannot silently convert a BREACH to PASS;
- evidence and rationale are retained.
