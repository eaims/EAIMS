# Financial Transparency

Before accepting public donations or organizational sponsorship, EAIMS will publish the receiving legal person or entity, payment channels, fee treatment, decision authority, permitted uses, restricted-fund handling, refund policy, reporting period, and applicable tax limitations.

An annual report should disclose opening balance, receipts by category, material sponsors, expenditures by category, restricted balances, in-kind contributions, related-party transactions, closing balance, and funded deliverables.

No donation is tax-deductible unless the receiving entity is legally authorized to make that representation.
