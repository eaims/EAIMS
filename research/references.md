# Research and Reference Foundations

EAIMS should evolve through documented evidence and public review.

1. NIST, *Artificial Intelligence Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*, NIST AI 100-1, 2023. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1
2. NIST, *Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile*, NIST AI 600-1, 2024. https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1
3. ISO/IEC 42001:2023, *Artificial intelligence — Management system*. https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
4. ISO/IEC 23894:2023, *Artificial intelligence — Guidance on risk management*. https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html
5. OECD, *Recommendation of the Council on Artificial Intelligence*, OECD/LEGAL/0449, updated 2024. https://legalinstruments.oecd.org/en/instruments/OECD-LEGAL-0449
6. European Union, *Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)*. https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng
7. OWASP Foundation, *Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications*. https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/

These sources inform research but do not imply endorsement, equivalence, certification, alignment, or conformance. EAIMS does not reproduce proprietary standards text.

Contributors should prefer primary standards, official guidance, peer-reviewed research, and transparent empirical studies. Proprietary maturity models may inform comparative research but must not be copied or represented as EAIMS content.

Normative requirement-to-source traceability is planned for v0.2. Until pilot validation is published, maturity anchors are evidence-informed hypotheses open to revision, not validated universal thresholds.
