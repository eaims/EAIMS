This file defines role semantics; it is not a list of public personal names.

## Role labels

EAIMS may record non-personal role identifiers such as:

- `contributor`;
- `contributor of findings`;
- `reviewer`;
- `external assessor`;
- `feasibility-probe author`;
- `collaborator`;
- `implementation participant`; or
- `research input source`.

A role label records only the function actually performed. It does not imply broader status.

## No automatic rights from participation

No contributor, reviewer, assessor, collaborator, or external participant acquires, solely by participation:

- EAIMS authorship or co-authorship;
- ownership or co-ownership in pre-existing EAIMS intellectual property;
- governance, voting, veto, approval, or release rights;
- maintainership or stewardship;
- licensing, sublicensing, or relicensing authority;
- trademark or branding rights; or
- authority to represent or bind EAIMS.

## Direct incorporation of third-party material

If third-party substantive text, code, schema, test, translation, graphic, or other copyrightable artifact is to be directly incorporated into an official EAIMS release, the mandatory CLA/copyright-assignment requirements apply before incorporation.

Use of a finding, idea, factual observation, or recommendation as input to independently drafted EAIMS material does not by itself make the source an EAIMS author or co-owner.
