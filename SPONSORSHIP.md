# Sponsorship Policy

Support may fund research, tooling, pilots, accessibility, translations, sector profiles, infrastructure, and project operations.

Funding cannot purchase specification changes, favorable scores, certification, accreditation, endorsement, exclusive rights, confidential benchmark access, governance control, or suppression of negative results.

Material sponsors, purpose restrictions, in-kind support, conflicts, and funded outputs must be disclosed. Sponsored research follows the same RFC and review rules as other work. Maintainers may reject funding that threatens neutrality or trust.
