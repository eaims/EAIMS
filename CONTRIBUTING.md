# Contributing to EAIMS — v1.0

EAIMS welcomes issues, factual corrections, review findings, research references, implementation feedback, and proposed improvements. Contribution does not create authorship, co-ownership, governance authority, veto rights, release-approval rights, or official-representative status.

## Before submitting

Do not submit confidential, employer-owned, client-owned, proprietary, paywalled, restricted, or unlawfully copied material. Do not reproduce protected wording, tables, graphics, taxonomies, or scoring rules from third-party standards or proprietary frameworks without documented permission.

## Issues, findings, and ideas

Issues and review findings may describe facts, defects, ideas, requirements, or recommendations. When EAIMS uses such input but independently drafts the final expression, the submitter remains in the factual/review role actually performed and does not become an EAIMS author or co-owner merely because the input informed a change.

## Pull requests and substantive contributions

A pull request is a proposal, not an accepted part of an official release.

**Before any third-party Substantive Contribution is incorporated into an official EAIMS release, the contributor MUST complete a project-approved CLA or copyright assignment in accordance with `IP_POLICY.md`, `CONTRIBUTOR-RIGHTS.md`, `CLA.md`, and `COPYRIGHT-ASSIGNMENT.md`.**

**No third-party Substantive Contribution SHALL be incorporated into an official EAIMS release until that required rights instrument is fully completed and accepted for rights-administration purposes.**

DCO sign-off may also be required for provenance, but DCO sign-off does not replace the mandatory CLA/assignment requirement for a Substantive Contribution.

The project SHALL refuse or postpone incorporation while required chain of title, employer authority, confidentiality, third-party rights, patent permissions, or relicensing rights remain unresolved. Where a sole-ownership chain is required for the contribution, the project copyright assignment—not a non-assignment CLA—must be completed.

## Sign-off

Where the DCO process applies, commits should include a `Signed-off-by` line consistent with `DCO.md`. The legal name and contact information used in a private CLA or assignment need not be repeated in public release documentation unless legally required or expressly approved for publication.

## Editorial and release acceptance

Technical review, passing tests, or approval by a reviewer does not by itself constitute editorial acceptance or release approval. Final official-release acceptance is governed by `GOVERNANCE.md`.

## Attribution

Public attribution follows `ATTRIBUTION-POLICY.md`. A contributor may be identified in legally required records or private rights instruments without being represented publicly as an EAIMS author, co-author, steward, maintainer, governing authority, or official representative.
