# Contributing to EAIMS

Contributions may improve capabilities, questions, evidence criteria, sector profiles, research, translations, tooling, examples, accessibility, or clarity.

## Process

1. Search existing issues.
2. Open a proposal for material changes.
3. Explain the problem, evidence, and compatibility impact.
4. Submit a focused pull request.
5. Respond to review.

## Rights and sign-off

Every commit MUST include `Signed-off-by: Full Name <email@example.com>`.

By signing, contributors certify [DCO.md](DCO.md), license the contribution under the license applicable to its destination, and confirm the right to submit it. Contributors MUST identify third-party material and disclose material AI assistance.

Contributions must be vendor-neutral, evidence-informed, clear, testable, and independently written. Do not submit confidential employer or client data, undisclosed promotional content, fabricated evidence, proprietary framework content, paid standards text, or unauthorized certification claims.
