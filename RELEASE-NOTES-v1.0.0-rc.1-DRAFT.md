# EAIMS 1.0.0-rc.1 — Draft Release Notes

**Status: DRAFT — not yet released**

EAIMS 1.0 redesigns the research baseline into an evidence-grounded and executable enterprise AI maturity specification focused on value, evidence, autonomy, accountability and operational risk.

## Highlights

- 8 dimensions and 30 canonical capabilities.
- 5 capability-specific maturity levels with 150 observable anchors.
- 206 normative/cross-cutting requirements in the FC16 candidate.
- Human Accountability Boundary and Human Reserved decisions.
- A0–A5 autonomy classification and Agent Permission Envelope controls.
- Model/System Sourcing Profile and external-change exposure handling.
- Evidence provenance, validity, cutoff, counter-evidence and conflict semantics.
- Explicit risk/gate architecture for material, high-impact and agentic AI.
- Capability-specific anchor eligibility and cross-capability consistency checks.
- Assessment Quality and structured multi-assessor disagreement resolution.
- Three end-to-end **synthetic** reference implementations.
- Machine-readable specification, schemas, executable validator/tests and Docker/Compose reference packaging.

## Validation boundary

The candidate demonstrates deterministic design and synthetic/reference behavior. It does **not** yet establish broad empirical validity, inter-rater reliability across organizations, regulatory conformity, certification status, or production deployment validation.

## Field-informed design statement

The redesign is informed by feasibility testing, synthetic reference implementations, and anonymized enterprise pilot-readiness inputs. These inputs must not be represented as named production deployments or endorsements.

## Before this draft can become rc.1

Hosted runtime CI, container/Compose evidence, release-environment security/SBOM evidence, and independent RC review exit criteria must be completed.
