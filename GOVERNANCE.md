# EAIMS Governance

## Mission

Maintain EAIMS as an open, credible, vendor-neutral, and evidence-informed enterprise AI maturity standard.

## Roles

### Maintainers
- Approve releases
- Manage repository quality
- Resolve contribution disputes
- Protect neutrality and integrity

### Contributors
- Submit issues, research, improvements, examples, translations, or tools

### Reviewers
- Review proposals in defined subject areas

### Advisory Council
A future non-executive body representing industry, academia, public sector, risk, engineering, and civil society.

## Decision process

1. Proposal submitted as an issue or pull request
2. Public review
3. Maintainer assessment
4. Documented decision
5. Release-note inclusion for material changes

## Conflict of interest

Maintainers and reviewers SHOULD disclose relevant commercial interests.

## Neutrality

No organization may purchase control over the standard.

Sponsorship MAY support project operations but MUST NOT guarantee standard changes, endorsement, certification, or privileged scoring.

## Versioning

- `0.x` — community drafts
- `1.x` — stable compatible revisions
- `2.x` — material structural changes

## Trademark and implementation claims

Implementers MAY state compatibility with a named EAIMS version when they disclose modifications.

Only the project governance body may designate an official EAIMS release.
