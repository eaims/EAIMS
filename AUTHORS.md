## No implied co-authorship or shared authority

Being a contributor, reviewer, assessor, feasibility-probe author, collaborator, sponsor, external participant, implementation provider, or source of findings does **not**, by itself, create:

- EAIMS authorship or co-authorship;
- copyright ownership or co-ownership in pre-existing or independently authored EAIMS material;
- stewardship or co-stewardship;
- maintainer status;
- governance rights or voting rights;
- veto or approval rights;
- release authority;
- licensing or relicensing authority; or
- authority to act as an official EAIMS representative.

Any such role or authority must arise from an explicit written instrument or written governance delegation.

## Public-name policy

EAIMS 1.0 public release materials name no other individual as an EAIMS author, co-author, steward, maintainer, governing authority, or official representative unless Elias Naserkhaki later gives explicit approval for that specific role and publication context.

Legally required attribution, bibliographic citation, third-party notices, and external-review records are governed separately by `ATTRIBUTION-POLICY.md` and do not create EAIMS authorship or governance status.
