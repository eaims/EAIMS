# EAIMS Public Attribution Policy — v1.0

## 1. EAIMS authorship and official-role naming

Public EAIMS 1.0 release artifacts SHALL identify **Elias Naserkhaki** as the only named individual presented as an EAIMS author, co-author, steward, maintainer, governing authority, or official representative unless he later provides explicit approval for a specific additional person, role, and publication context.

No external reviewer, assessor, contributor, collaborator, source of findings, or other participant shall be elevated by implication from their actual role to EAIMS author, co-author, steward, maintainer, governing authority, or official representative.

## 2. Non-personal identifiers

Reviewers, assessors, enterprise design inputs, external collaborators, and synthetic actors SHOULD use non-personal identifiers such as `REV-001`, `ASM-001`, `EDC-A`, `COL-001`, or `ORG-A` in public release artifacts whenever personal identification is not legally or operationally necessary.

Public templates SHOULD NOT require a real person's name unless a legal process, rights instrument, or explicit publication decision requires it.

## 3. Required carve-outs

The no-additional-public-name rule does **not** prohibit a person's name when identification is reasonably necessary for one of the following:

- **legally required attribution or notice**;
- compliance with an applicable open-source, open-content, or third-party license;
- **bibliographic citation** of an external publication or source;
- identification of an external standard, legal authority, court, author, or source where normal citation practice requires it;
- **external review records** where naming is required by the review protocol, law, contract, publication venue, or an approved transparency decision;
- copyright notices, patent notices, moral-rights notices, or other **third-party material** that must preserve the original attribution; or
- private or legally required CLA, copyright-assignment, provenance, conflict-of-interest, or compliance records.

These carve-outs record the external person's actual legal, bibliographic, or review role only. They do **not** create EAIMS authorship, co-authorship, stewardship, maintainership, governance authority, ownership, co-ownership, release authority, or official-representative status.

## 4. Accurate role attribution

Where an external person's involvement is documented, the role SHALL be described accurately, for example `external assessor`, `feasibility-probe author`, `reviewer`, or `contributor of findings`.

Use of such a role label does not create ownership, authorship of EAIMS, governance rights, veto rights, approval rights, or authority to represent EAIMS.

If substantive copyrightable text, code, or an artifact from that person is directly incorporated into the repository or official release, the mandatory CLA/copyright-assignment requirements apply before incorporation.

## 5. No accidental attribution

Public generated outputs MUST NOT copy personal names from internal review, assessment, or rights-management records into release artifacts unless the publication is required under Section 3 or explicitly approved by Elias Naserkhaki.

## 6. Historical platform records

This policy governs EAIMS 1.0 release materials and future public artifacts. It does not assert that immutable historical commit metadata, legal records, or third-party platform history can or should always be rewritten.
