This document is a release-control policy and is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## 1. Proposal status

Issues, comments, reviews, pull requests, patches, translations, examples, tests, schemas, data structures, documentation, and other submissions are proposals. Submission, review, or acceptance for discussion does not guarantee incorporation into an official EAIMS release.

## 2. Substantive contributions require a signed rights instrument

A Substantive Contribution has the meaning defined in `IP_POLICY.md`.

**A Substantive Contribution from a third party MUST NOT be incorporated into an official EAIMS release unless the required CLA or copyright assignment has been completed before incorporation.**

DCO sign-off alone is insufficient for this purpose.

Where the project requires a sole-ownership chain for official copyrightable expression, the contributor MUST execute the project copyright assignment. A CLA may be used where approved by Elias Naserkhaki and where it grants sufficiently broad, irrevocable rights for use, modification, reproduction, distribution, sublicensing, relicensing, dual-licensing, commercial licensing, proprietary relicensing, and any contributor-controlled patent permissions required by the applicable form. A CLA may leave copyright ownership of the identified Contribution with the contributor; that does not create joint ownership of EAIMS as a whole, co-authorship of EAIMS, or governance authority.

## 3. Contributor representations

Before incorporation, a contributor MUST represent that:

- the contribution is original to the contributor or lawfully controlled by the contributor;
- the contributor has authority to grant the rights stated in the applicable agreement;
- the contribution does not knowingly violate employer, client, confidentiality, contractual, or third-party rights;
- all material third-party dependencies or encumbrances are disclosed; and
- no undisclosed agreement prevents the rights granted to the EAIMS project from being exercised.

## 4. Feedback and findings without incorporation

A reviewer, assessor, feasibility-probe author, collaborator, or other external participant may provide a finding, observation, defect report, requirement, idea, fact, or recommendation. EAIMS may use the uncopyrightable idea, fact, or finding as input and independently draft EAIMS expression.

Such participation does not itself create authorship, co-authorship, ownership, co-ownership, governance rights, veto rights, approval rights, maintainership, or official-representative status.

If the participant's copyrightable expression, code, or artifact is copied or directly incorporated, the mandatory rights-instrument rule applies.

## 5. No implied partnership or authority

Submitting or accepting a contribution does not create partnership, employment, agency, fiduciary status, co-ownership of pre-existing EAIMS material, governance authority, licensing authority, release authority, or authority to bind EAIMS.

## 6. Rejection and clean-room rewrite

If the required rights cannot be obtained, the contribution SHALL remain outside the official release. The project may independently rewrite a non-copyrightable idea, fact, requirement, or functional concept without copying protected expression.
