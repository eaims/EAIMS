# Reviewer Program

EAIMS invites named, consenting reviewers in enterprise architecture, AI engineering, data, lifecycle operations, governance, security, responsible AI, organizational change, measurement, accessibility, localization, and sector practice.

Reviewers must disclose relevant employment, clients, investments, sponsorships, authored competing models, and commercial implementations. Review participation does not imply endorsement. Reviewers do not receive certification authority or privileged scoring.

The project will list a reviewer only after explicit consent and a substantive public or documented review. This file intentionally contains no invented advisory board or implied affiliations.
