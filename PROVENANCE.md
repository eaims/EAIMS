# EAIMS v1.0 Provenance and Rights Classification

Copyright © 2026 Elias Naserkhaki. All rights reserved except as expressly licensed.

This record separates provenance classes so that authorship, ownership, public licensing, third-party rights, external input, trademarks, and governance are not conflated.

## P1 — Original EAIMS material

Original EAIMS specification text, capability/anchor structures, requirements, schemas, reference-engine implementation, tests, documentation, and synthetic reference materials are treated as EAIMS-originated material unless a file or provenance record states otherwise. Ownership applies only to the extent lawfully held; public license grants are stated in `LICENSE`.

## P2 — Previously released EAIMS material

Legacy EAIMS material reused for compatibility remains subject to public-license grants already made for the applicable released version. Later policy does not retroactively revoke those grants.

## P3 — Third-party software dependencies and tools

External packages, container images, CI actions, and development tools referenced by package/configuration files are not treated as EAIMS-authored content merely because they are dependencies. They remain under their own rights and licenses. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

## P4 — External findings, reviews, and design inputs

External findings, observations, facts, ideas, recommendations, and feasibility inputs may inform independently drafted EAIMS expression. The role of the external source is not converted into EAIMS authorship, co-ownership, or governance authority. Direct incorporation of third-party copyrightable expression requires the mandatory rights instrument defined in `IP_POLICY.md`.

## P5 — External citations and third-party materials

Bibliographic citations, legally required attributions, license notices, and third-party material must retain their required provenance. Their inclusion does not make the external source an EAIMS author, steward, or governing authority.

## P6 — Trademarks and project identity

EAIMS names, logos, visual identity, domains, official-release designations, and project goodwill are governed separately by `TRADEMARK.md` and are not granted by the repository copyright licenses.

## P7 — Governance authority

Governance and official-release authority are defined separately in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Public licensing, citation, review, contribution, or implementation does not create governance authority.
