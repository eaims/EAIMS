# EAIMS Pilot Protocol v0.2

## Objective

Test clarity, feasibility, evidence availability, scoring consistency, decision usefulness, and burden—not prove universal validity.

## Minimum pilot design

- Three or more organizations across at least two contexts
- Two assessors independently score a shared subset where permitted
- Predefined scope, consent, confidentiality, and withdrawal process
- Recorded completion time, unclear anchors, missing evidence, disagreements, and gate effects
- No public organization identification without written authorization

## Reliability analysis

Report exact agreement, adjacent agreement, weighted Cohen’s kappa where assumptions and sample permit, disagreement by capability, and confidence-level differences. Do not report a single reliability statistic without sample size and interpretation limits.

## Change control

Convert findings into public issues or RFCs using anonymized descriptions. Record whether each change clarifies language, changes scoring meaning, adds evidence guidance, or breaks compatibility.

## Publication

Publish protocol, sample description, conflicts, funding, results, limitations, changes, and unresolved questions. Negative results and disagreement are valuable evidence and must not be hidden.
