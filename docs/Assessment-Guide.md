# EAIMS Assessment Guide

## Recommended phases

### Phase 1 — Initiate
- Confirm sponsor and scope
- Identify business units and systems
- Select self, facilitated, or independent assessment
- Establish confidentiality and evidence handling rules

### Phase 2 — Discover
- Conduct stakeholder interviews
- Collect documents, metrics, and system evidence
- Record evidence confidence
- Identify material AI systems and dependencies

### Phase 3 — Score
- Score each capability
- Resolve disagreements through evidence
- Apply critical gates
- Document assumptions and exceptions

### Phase 4 — Validate
- Review scores with capability owners
- Challenge unsupported optimism
- Separate current state from aspirations

### Phase 5 — Roadmap
- Prioritize gaps using business value, risk, urgency, dependency, and effort
- Define target maturity by dimension
- Assign owners and measurable outcomes

### Phase 6 — Reassess
- Track evidence and outcomes
- Reassess after agreed milestones
