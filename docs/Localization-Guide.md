# EAIMS Localization Guide

Localized implementations MAY translate language and adapt regulatory references, sector terminology, examples, evidence expectations, risk profiles, and industry weights.

Localized implementations MUST NOT change maturity-level meanings without disclosure, remove critical gates while claiming unmodified conformance, present commercial claims as EAIMS research, or imply endorsement, certification authority, or governance privilege.

Commercial platforms SHOULD state:

> This product independently implements or adapts EAIMS. It is not the EAIMS project and is not endorsed or certified by EAIMS.

Commercial and noncommercial implementations are separate projects permitted on equal license terms.
