# EAIMS Capability Matrix v0.1
Each capability is assessed from Level 0 through Level 5. Evidence must support the assigned level.

## 1. Strategy and Leadership

### 1.1 AI vision and strategic alignment
| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 0 | No recognized AI opportunity or strategic discussion. |
| 1 | Informal executive interest and isolated experimentation. |
| 2 | Department-level objectives and prioritized use cases exist. |
| 3 | Approved enterprise AI strategy, owners, funding, and roadmap. |
| 4 | Strategy is continuously adjusted using outcomes, risk, and market intelligence. |
| 5 | AI is embedded in enterprise strategy and operating-model design. |

### 1.2 Executive ownership and governance
| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 0 | No accountable owner. |
| 1 | Individual champions without formal authority. |
| 2 | Department sponsors and steering activities exist. |
| 3 | Executive accountability and cross-functional governance are defined. |
| 4 | Governance manages the enterprise AI portfolio and trade-offs. |
| 5 | AI accountability is embedded in corporate governance. |

### 1.3 Policy and decision rights
| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 0 | No AI policies or decision rights. |
| 1 | Informal guidance and inconsistent approvals. |
| 2 | Basic acceptable-use and project approval rules. |
| 3 | Enterprise policies and decision-right matrices are operational. |
| 4 | Policies are risk-tiered, measured, and regularly improved. |
| 5 | Policy enforcement and assurance are integrated into adaptive operations. |
