# Assessment Quality — FC16

Assessment Quality is a diagnostic of the **assessment process**, not the organization. It never directly changes maturity. FC16 uses three transparent levels: `HIGH`, `MODERATE`, and `LIMITED`.

Factors include scope completeness, evidence coverage, critical-requirement coverage, evidence confidence, unresolved evidence/assessor conflicts, stale or invalid evidence, and assessor independence. The thresholds in `spec/assessment-quality.yaml` are **design-calibrated and provisional** pending empirical study.

A self-assessment can be useful, but under FC16 it cannot receive `HIGH` Assessment Quality solely from completeness and evidence strength; `HIGH` requires at least internal-independent, external, or multi-assessor review.

A high maturity result may coexist with `LIMITED` Assessment Quality. In that case the maturity result is retained but its assurance limitation must be visible.
